QuotaVadis 0.6.3

• Settings → Visual theme is now one picker: Auto (default), Default, Observatory, GoodLimits.ai. Your previous Observatory setting carries over.
• New optional GoodLimits.ai look in the GoodData brand (black, lime and grey, with the GoodData spiral) for the menu bar panel, About window, iPhone app and widgets.
• Auto picks GoodLimits.ai by itself when one of your tracked accounts is a GoodData one (an @gooddata.com email, or the Claude organization "gooddata" / "gooddata.ai"); otherwise it keeps the default look. No extra network calls, and a manual choice always wins.
• Under GoodLimits.ai the Dock / ⌘-Tab icon switches while the app runs; on iPhone the app icon switches too (iOS asks once to confirm).
• Pace widget and small widgets keep their numbers readable on the light GoodLimits.ai background.
