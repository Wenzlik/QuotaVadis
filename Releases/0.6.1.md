QuotaVadis 0.6.1

• Settings, Usage details, Welcome and About now open as normal app windows: QuotaVadis shows in the Dock and ⌘-Tab while one is open, so you can switch back to a window after another app covers it. Closing the last window (⌘W or the close button) returns QuotaVadis to the menu bar only.
• Opening Settings or Usage details from the menu always brings the window to the front with focus.
• Under the hood: the limit overview, risk summary and forecast logic are now shared with the iPhone app, which gets the same forecasts, main bar and "Most at risk" summary (no visible change on the Mac).
