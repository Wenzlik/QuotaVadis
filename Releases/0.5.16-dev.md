QuotaVadis 0.5.16-dev (Development channel — experimental)

• Pace and forecast: every limit bar now shows a pace marker (where you would be at an even rate) with "X% under / over pace", plus a forecast from your recent usage — "fits, ends at ~78%" or "runs out at 14:20 · 40 min before reset", coloured by risk.
• Risk summary at the top of the panel: the closest limit and how many limits are at risk before their reset.
• New layout: a large bar for the 5-hour session window, with weekly and other limits as smaller cards below it.
• Widgets: after an update the stale widget extension is restarted, so desktop widgets no longer stay black.
• iPhone Duo / iPad: the Limits tab shows two panes on the inner display — providers on the left, detail on the right.
• Cleanup: unified usage colour thresholds across the panel, menu bar, widgets and CLI; dead code removed.
• Only offered on the Development update channel (Settings ▸ Updates). Stable users stay on 0.5.15.
