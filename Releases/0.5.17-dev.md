QuotaVadis 0.5.17-dev (Development channel — experimental)

• Panel header now shows the limit most at risk: limits that run out before their reset come first, then the highest usage.
• Choose the main bar per provider: Settings ▸ General ▸ Main bar, or right-click a provider card ▸ Show as main bar (e.g. Cursor → Grok Bot).
• One colour rule: bars and percentages follow the forecast (fits green, tight orange, runs out red), everywhere in the panel and the dashboard.
• Spend over its cap (e.g. Cursor on-demand) counts as at risk.
• Small cards no longer cut off the reset and forecast lines (shorter labels, up to two lines).
• Better contrast for red and orange text on the glass cards.
• Only offered on the Development update channel (Settings ▸ Updates). Stable users stay on 0.5.15.
