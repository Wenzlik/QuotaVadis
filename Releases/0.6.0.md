QuotaVadis 0.6.0

• Pace and forecast: every limit bar shows a pace marker with "X% under / over pace" and a forecast to the reset — "fits, ends at ~78%" or "runs out at 14:20 · 40 min before reset". Without enough fresh data it honestly says "forecast: not enough data yet".
• One colour rule: bars and percentages follow the forecast risk (fits green, tight orange, runs out red), in the panel and the dashboard; text stays readable on the glass panel.
• "Most at risk" summary: the limit closest to running out and how many limits are at risk before their reset. Shown at the bottom of the panel by default — choose Bottom, Top or Hidden in Settings ▸ General.
• New layout: a large bar for the 5-hour session window, weekly and other limits as smaller cards below; choose the main bar per provider (Settings ▸ General ▸ Main bar, or right-click a provider card).
• Spend over its cap (e.g. Cursor on-demand) counts as at risk.
• Widgets: after an update the stale widget extension is restarted, so desktop widgets no longer stay black.
• iPhone Duo / iPad: the Limits tab shows two panes on the inner display.
