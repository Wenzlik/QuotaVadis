QuotaVadis 0.6.1-dev (Development channel — experimental)

• Settings, Usage details, Welcome and About now open as normal app windows: QuotaVadis shows in the Dock and ⌘-Tab while one is open, so you can switch back to it after another app covers it. Closing the last window (⌘W or the close button) returns it to the menu bar only.
• Opening Settings from the menu now always brings the window to the front with focus.
• Under the hood: the limit overview, risk summary and forecast logic are shared with the iPhone app (no visible change on the Mac).
• Only offered on the Development update channel (Settings ▸ Updates). Stable users stay on 0.6.0.
