QuotaVadis 0.6.0-dev (Development channel — experimental)

• Forecast fix: "fits, ends at ~X%" now projects usage to the reset (current + rate × time left) instead of repeating the current value. Without enough fresh data it says "forecast: not enough data yet".
• The panel summary no longer claims "All on track" for limits without a forecast.
• "Most at risk" summary moved to the bottom of the panel; choose Bottom, Top or Hidden in Settings ▸ General.
• Red, orange and green text is readable on the glass panel without dark backing pills; the big header percentage follows the forecast colour too.
• Only offered on the Development update channel (Settings ▸ Updates). Stable users stay on 0.5.15.
