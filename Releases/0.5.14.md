QuotaVadis 0.5.14

• Menu bar (Filled bars): new circular badges — a dark centre with white digits and the usage colour on a proportional arc around the rim, so the number keeps the same contrast at any percentage. Missing data shows "–" (inside or beside the badge) instead of looking like 0.
• Settings ▸ Menu Bar ▸ Style: Filled bars has a new "Bar look" choice: Circular (default) or Classic (the original capsule pills, unchanged). The menu bar switches instantly and the choice is remembered. Czech labels: Kruhové / Původní.
• Stronger monochrome vendor marks on circular badges; the short-window (5h) mark stays slightly smaller. The stale indicator dot is no longer clipped at the right edge.
